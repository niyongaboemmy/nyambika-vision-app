v1 patterns we remove

🔍 Search box & keywords
📋 Category lists & filters
🧱 Product grids to scroll
📷 Photo upload for every try-on
🧾 Multi-step checkout forms
🗂 Tab bar with 5 sections

v2 design principles

🎯 Intent over searchSay or show what you need: occasion, budget, fabric.
🪞 You firstEverything is shown on your private twin, not on a model.
👆 Gestures over buttonsSwipe, drag, pinch, hold. Almost no forms.
🎙 Conversational & ambientThe Muse speaks RW/EN/FR. Updates come to you.
🧊 Spatial3D, AR mirror and Maker Spaces instead of flat pages.
🧵 Makers at the centreEvery look is attributed. Pay the maker directly.
